# Machine-voice patterns and how to fix them

Sources: [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [blader/humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), [brandonwise/humanizer](https://github.com/brandonwise/humanizer), and Vietnamese guides such as [quantrimang.com](https://quantrimang.com/meo-nhan-biet-van-ban-do-ai-tao-ra-214574). These are signs to reread and fix, **not proof** of authorship. One sign alone is nearly worthless; several together are worth a look. In real technical writing, benefit sentences and semicolons are common, so do not delete them just because they are on this list.

| Group | Sign | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Staging | "In today's landscape...", "It is important to note", "Trong bối cảnh...", "Cần lưu ý rằng" | Drop the lead-in, state the point |
| False contrast | "not only X but also Y", "không chỉ X mà còn Y" when nobody claimed X | State what you mean, drop the negative half |
| Echo closer | "In conclusion", "Tóm lại", a last sentence restating the paragraph | Cut, or replace with a new fact |
| Inflated significance | "plays a crucial role", "đóng vai trò quan trọng", "the future looks bright" | Say what it concretely does |
| Vagueness | "various aspects", "nhiều khía cạnh", "các yếu tố khác nhau" | Give a figure, name or example |
| Even rhythm | Sentences of equal length, same openers | Merge or split, vary the opening |
| Forced triads | Exactly three items to sound complete | Keep the real number |
| Connector openers | "Moreover", "Hơn nữa", "Ngoài ra" in a row | Drop them, let meaning link the sentences |
| Em dashes | "—" everywhere | Comma, parentheses, or split the sentence |
| Chatbot residue | "I hope this helps", "Bạn có muốn tôi...", "oaicite", "utm_source=chatgpt" | Delete |
| Formatting | Bold on every label, Title Case Headings, emoji | Remove needless formatting |
| Benefit sentence | "This avoids situations where..." after every description | Keep only if the cause is real in the code; else cut |

Stock words to watch (English): delve, tapestry, crucial, pivotal, landscape, testament, seamless, robust.
