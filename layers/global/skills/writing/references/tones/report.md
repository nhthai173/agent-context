# Tone: report, proposal, summary for a decision maker

**Reader:** a manager or partner with little time who does not need implementation detail. **Goal:** they can decide after the first paragraph.

- Open with the conclusion or the ask in one sentence, then the key numbers, then detail.
- Say what you need from the reader and by when.
- Numbers carry units and a date; compare with a baseline (plan, last month).
- State risks and unknowns plainly with a mitigation; do not bury them in long paragraphs.
- Drop internal jargon, or explain it once at first use.
- One page at most; push detail into an appendix.
- Polite and direct; avoid hype ("outstanding", "groundbreaking") unless backed by a number.

Before/after:
- Before: "Overall, the project is progressing positively and bringing many benefits."
- After: "The project delivered 8 of 10 agreed items; 2 are one week late waiting on a license. Request: approve 5 more person-days before Nov 15."
