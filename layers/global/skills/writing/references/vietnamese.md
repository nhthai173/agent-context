# Vietnamese notes

Write Vietnamese for a Vietnamese reader: do not mix English sentences into Vietnamese ones. Keep technical terms that have no natural translation (token, webhook, backend, migration) and all identifiers as they are.

## Calques seen in practice (and the fix)
- "candidate position" rendered as "vị trí ứng viên": sounds odd. Use "các vị trí cần xét" or "vị trí dự kiến", or drop the concept and say the action ("lần lượt từng vị trí trong khu vực").
- "owner/lead of the step" rendered as "đối tượng chủ trì": use "thành phần trực tiếp thực thi".
- "implement" rendered as "cài đặt": in a thesis prefer "hiện thực" or "triển khai", consistently.
- "hoàn thành nhanh", "kéo dài" are colloquial in formal text: use "trong thời gian ngắn", "đòi hỏi thời gian dài hơn".

## Style
- Prefer active verbs over "việc + verb" nominalizations and chained "được ... được".
- Stock Vietnamese connectors at the start of consecutive sentences ("Hơn nữa", "Ngoài ra", "Bên cạnh đó") are a machine-voice sign; drop them and let the sentences link by meaning.
- In Markdown write one line per paragraph or bullet; never hard-wrap.
